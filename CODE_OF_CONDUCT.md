# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ShakerScan's issues, discussions and other community spaces to be welcoming, respectful
and useful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith, and focus on the problem rather than the person.
- Share security-sensitive details only through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Respect other people's systems: never post data, credentials, or findings from systems you are
  not authorized to test or disclose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Encouraging or facilitating unauthorized testing of systems.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock or close threads, and block accounts that
violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/andriyze) or, for anything security-related, through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
